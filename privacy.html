Privacy Policy

Healthy Tracker is a private personal health-tracking application.

Data we use

With the user's explicit Google authorization, the application may read health and fitness data available through Google Health API, including sleep, steps and activity, exercise sessions, heart-rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, and other supported health metrics needed for the personal tracker.

How data is used

The data is used only to display and analyze the user's own health and activity history in Healthy Tracker.

Sharing

Healthy Tracker does not sell health data and does not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Storage and access

The application requests read-only access to Google Health data. The user can revoke access through their Google account at any time.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, use the contact information associated with the Healthy Tracker application in Google Cloud.
